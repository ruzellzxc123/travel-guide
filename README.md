# "The Ultimate Guide to PH".

This is widely considered the "Sweet Spot" for travelers.

Weather: It is the "cool" dry season. You get bright blue skies and sunshine, but the air is much more comfortable for walking around cities like Manila or hiking in the Cordilleras.

Vibe: Very festive. This window covers Christmas, New Year, and major festivals like Sinulog (Cebu) and Ati-Atihan (Aklan) in January.

Heads up: This is peak tourist season. Prices for hotels and flights are at their highest, and popular spots like Boracay or El Nido will be crowded.
LOCALSNACKS:

Taho: Fresh soft tofu with arnibal (sweet syrup) and sago pearls.
Kwek-kwek: Deep-fried quail eggs in a bright orange batter.
Isaw: Grilled chicken or pork intestines on skewers.
Turon: Banana (saba) wrapped in spring roll wrapper and fried with brown sugar.
Fish Balls/Kikiam: Deep-fried street-style fish or meat balls served with sauces.
